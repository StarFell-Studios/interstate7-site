A 17 year old started it to prove a point.

In 1996 the local kids kept getting sponsored by companies we thought were lame. So we made our own. Interstate 7 put cartoon characters on the front of shirts and hats and sponsored our friends ourselves.

Carl was there from the start. He is a reaper in a red hoodie who never hurts anybody and always shows up a little too happy. He and his crew got drawn and redrawn until about 2004, and then the road went quiet for twenty years.

It just opened back up.

Carl doesn't take lives. He just shows up early.
